RESEARCH LIMITATIONS
-------------------------------------------------------
The experiment uses a balanced 200-image CIFAR-10 test sample and a randomly selected 10,000-image training subset. Training is limited to two epochs. Results are preliminary and do not establish general adversarial robustness. The same attack settings are used for model comparison, but stronger attacks and additional evaluation are needed.
