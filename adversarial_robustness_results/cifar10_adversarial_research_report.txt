RESEARCH NOTES
Attack success rate is calculated among images correctly classified before the attack.
Adversarial accuracy is calculated over the full selected test sample.
Perturbations are applied in raw pixel space with values constrained to [0, 1].
ResNet-50 uses differentiable tensor resizing to 224x224 during attack evaluation.
These results use a balanced 200-image sample and should not be interpreted as full test-set robustness measurements.
The experiment evaluates empirical robustness against the specified attacks, not guaranteed robustness against all adversarial attacks.
