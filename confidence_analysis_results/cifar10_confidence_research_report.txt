INTERPRETATION NOTES
Lower ECE indicates closer agreement between confidence and observed accuracy for the chosen bins.
Lower multiclass Brier score indicates better probability predictions.
Predictive entropy measures uncertainty in the model's probability distribution. It is not a standalone measure of calibration.
High-confidence errors identify incorrect predictions made with at least 90% softmax confidence.
These are test-set measurements. They do not guarantee performance on new or out-of-distribution images.
